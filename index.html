常見問題 / Frequently asked questions

BNI Anchor 是什麼？ / What is BNI Anchor?

BNI Anchor 是香港的 BNI 星期四分會，2025 年 11 月 6 日成立。會員貫徹 Givers Gain，互相引薦和轉介，尋求長期生意和合作機會。 BNI Anchor is a Thursday BNI chapter in Hong Kong, founded on 6 November 2025. Members build long-term business relationships through introductions and referrals.

BNI Anchor 在哪裡聚會？ / Where does BNI Anchor meet?

商務會議時間和地址請留意 BNI Anchor 的 Facebook 及 Instagram 專頁。 Check BNI Anchor’s Facebook and Instagram pages for the current meeting time and venue.

如何以訪客身份參加商務會議？ / How can I visit a meeting?

在網站填寫查詢表單，提交後會開啟 WhatsApp；請確認內容並自行發送至 +852 6698 9778，由分會司庫跟進商務會議預約。 Complete the enquiry form, then review and send the prepared WhatsApp message to +852 6698 9778 to arrange a visitor meeting with the chapter treasurer.

如何尋找會員或專業服務？ / How can I find a member or service?

可按姓名、公司、行業或業務介紹搜尋會員，亦可用業務類別篩選，點選會員卡片查看詳細資料。 Search the directory by name, company, profession or introduction, or filter by business category. Select a member card for details.
